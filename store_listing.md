# Store Listing — Stack Tower

## English
**Short description (80 chars):**
Tap to stack blocks as high as you can! Simple, addictive, one-tap fun.

**Full description:**
How high can you build? Stack Tower is the ultimate one-tap stacking game.

Tap to drop each moving block on top of the tower. Anything that hangs over the edge gets sliced off — so the tower gets narrower with every miss. Nail a PERFECT drop to earn bonus coins, and land three perfects in a row to grow your block back!

FEATURES
• One-tap controls — easy to learn, hard to master
• Satisfying slicing physics and smooth 60 fps animation
• Speed increases as you climb higher
• Beautiful color gradients that change as your tower grows
• Earn coins and unlock block styles and color themes
• Beat your best score again and again
• Play offline, anytime

Tap. Stack. Repeat. Start building your tower now!
